# How to Use the My Account Page

To open your account page, select the account icon in the top-right corner of the navigation bar, then select **My Account**. This page shows your total rewards paid, any pending payouts, and lets you update your profile details.

---

## Overview

| Section | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Stat cards** | Summary of total rewards paid and pending payouts |
| **Profile Information** | Set your display name, phone, and country |
| **Payout History** | All past payout requests with status and transaction links |

---

## Step 1: Navigate to My Account

Select the account icon in the top-right corner, then select **My Account** from the dropdown.

![The account dropdown open in the top-right, showing the wallet address and "My Account" option.](/screenshots/gs-06-01_my-account-nav.png)

---

## Step 2: Read the stat cards

Two summary cards appear at the top:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| **Total Rewards Paid** | Cumulative USDC received across all settled payouts |
| **Pending Payouts** | Number of payout requests currently awaiting processing |

![Summary cards: Total Rewards Paid ($2,560.00) and Pending Payouts (0).](/screenshots/gs-06-02_stat-cards.png)

---

## Step 3: Update your profile

The **Profile Information** card lets you save your name, phone number, and country. Select **Edit**, fill in your details, and select **Save**.

![The Profile Information card in edit mode with Full Name, Phone, and Country fields. "Save" and "Cancel" buttons visible.](/screenshots/gs-06-03_profile-edit.png)

Note: Profile information is stored locally in your browser, keyed to your wallet address. It is not submitted on-chain.

---

## Step 4: View your payout history

The **Payout History** table on the right shows all payout requests for your wallet, including date, status, your 80% share, the firm's 20% cut, and a transaction link.

![The Payout History card with a table. Columns: Date, Status badge (Settled/Pending/Rejected), Profit 80%, Firm 20%, Transaction hash link.](/screenshots/gs-06-04_payout-history.png)

See [Payout History and Status →](../payouts/03-payout-history-and-status.md) for a full breakdown of payout statuses.
