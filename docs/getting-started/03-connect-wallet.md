# How to Connect Your Wallet

To use ChainFunded, select **Sign In** in the top-right corner of the navigation bar. Your wallet is your account — no email or registration required.

---

## Before you begin

- A Web3 wallet installed in your browser or on your phone. See [Supported Wallets →](./04-supported-wallets.md)
- Your wallet switched to **Ethereum Sepolia** (testnet)
- A small amount of Sepolia ETH for gas fees

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Open ChainFunded

Navigate to the ChainFunded app. You will land on the **Start Challenge** page by default.

![Full-width view of the Start Challenge page in dark mode. The five account size tier cards are visible. No wallet is connected — the top-right shows a "Sign In" button.](/screenshots/gs-03-01_landing-page.png)

---

### Step 2: Select "Sign In"

Select the **Sign In** button in the top-right corner of the navigation bar.

![The navigation bar showing the "Sign In" button on the right side, next to the "Start Challenge" button.](/screenshots/gs-03-02_connect-button.png)

---

### Step 3: Select your wallet

A modal appears with available wallet options. Select your wallet:
- Select **MetaMask** if you use the MetaMask browser extension
- Select **WalletConnect** to scan a QR code with your mobile wallet
- Select **Coinbase Wallet** if you use Coinbase Wallet

![The wallet connection modal showing MetaMask, WalletConnect, and Coinbase Wallet options on a dark background.](/screenshots/gs-03-03_wallet-modal.png)

---

### Step 4: Approve the connection in your wallet

Your wallet displays a connection request from ChainFunded. Review it and select **Connect** in your wallet popup.

![MetaMask popup showing the "Connect to ChainFunded" request with site name and the "Connect" button highlighted.](/screenshots/gs-03-04_metamask-approval.png)

Note: ChainFunded will never request a transaction at this step — only a connection approval. If you see a transaction request during connection, decline it and do not proceed.

---

### Step 5: Confirm you're connected

Once approved, a user icon appears in the top-right corner of the navigation bar. Select it to see your shortened wallet address (e.g., `0x1a2b…3c4d`) and account options.

![The navigation bar with the account dropdown open, showing the truncated wallet address and Sign Out option.](/screenshots/gs-03-05_connected-state.png)

You have now successfully connected your wallet to ChainFunded.

---

## Switching networks

If your wallet is on a different network, a warning banner appears. Select **Switch Network** in the banner and approve the switch to **Ethereum Sepolia** in your wallet.
