# What is ChainFunded?

ChainFunded is an on-chain prop trading evaluation platform. Traders pay a one-time fee to attempt a two-phase challenge. Those who pass receive a funded account and earn on-chain payouts — no intermediaries, no manual approval.

---

## How it works

1. **Pay the evaluation fee** — choose an account size ($10K–$200K) and pay in USDC
2. **Pass Phase 1** — hit a 10% profit target without violating drawdown rules
3. **Pass Phase 2** — hit a 5% profit target under the same drawdown rules
4. **Trade funded** — receive 80% of your profits as USDC, directly to your wallet

Every step is enforced by smart contracts on Ethereum. There is no manual review, no withdrawal queue, and no company holding your funds.

---

## Who is ChainFunded for?

- **Traders** who want access to a larger simulated account and on-chain payouts
- **Liquidity Providers (LPs)** who want to earn passive yield by backing the payout pool
- **Affiliates** who want to earn commissions by referring traders

---

## What makes it different?

| Feature | Traditional Prop Firms | ChainFunded |
|---------|----------------------|-------------|
| Payout processing | Manual, 1–14 days | Automatic, 30–90 seconds |
| Verification | Company decides | Smart contract rules |
| Capital custody | Firm holds funds | LP pool on-chain |
| Transparency | Opaque | All transactions on Etherscan |

---

## Key terms

- **Challenge** — the two-phase evaluation you trade to prove your edge
- **Funded account** — a live account you earn payouts from after passing both phases
- **CFND tokens** — LP tokens representing your share of the liquidity pool (1:1 with USDC)
- **Profit split** — 80% of profits go to the trader, 20% to the LP pool
