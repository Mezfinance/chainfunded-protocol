# Common Scams and How to Avoid Them

This article describes the most common scams targeting prop trading platform users, and how to identify and avoid them.

---

## Impersonation scams

**What it looks like:** Someone contacts you on Discord, Telegram, or Twitter claiming to be ChainFunded support, a moderator, or an admin. They offer to help with a problem, reset your account, or recover lost funds.

**The scam:** They will ask for your seed phrase, private key, or ask you to connect your wallet to a fake site they control.

**How to stay safe:**
- ChainFunded support will **never** contact you first via DM
- ChainFunded support will **never** ask for your seed phrase or private key
- Verify any support interaction by using the official support channel on the ChainFunded website

---

## Fake ChainFunded websites

**What it looks like:** A URL that looks nearly identical to the official ChainFunded domain (e.g., "chainfund3d.app", "chain-funded.io", "chainfunded-app.com"). The site looks identical to the real one.

**The scam:** When you connect your wallet, the site requests broad token approvals or a signature that drains your wallet.

**How to stay safe:**
- Bookmark the official ChainFunded URL and always navigate from the bookmark
- Double-check the URL in the address bar before connecting your wallet
- Never click links to ChainFunded from unsolicited messages

---

## Fake "reset" or "recovery" services

**What it looks like:** A post, ad, or message claiming they can reset a failed challenge for free, recover lost funds, or get you a discount on evaluation fees outside the platform.

**The scam:** These services either steal your funds directly or are outright fraudulent.

**How to stay safe:**
- All challenge management happens on the official ChainFunded platform
- There is no way to reset a challenge without paying the evaluation fee again
- No third party can access or modify your on-chain challenge data

---

## Malicious wallet approval requests

**What it looks like:** A transaction request in MetaMask asking you to approve "Unlimited" USDC spending, or a request you didn't initiate from a site you don't recognise.

**The scam:** An unlimited approval gives a contract the ability to drain your USDC at any time in the future.

**How to stay safe:**
- Never approve unlimited token spending unless you understand exactly why it's needed
- ChainFunded always requests approvals for specific amounts only
- Revoke unused approvals regularly using a token approval manager (e.g., Revoke.cash)

---

## Social media giveaway scams

**What it looks like:** A tweet, post, or ad claiming "ChainFunded is giving away funded accounts" or "send 0.1 ETH and get a $50,000 account". The account looks like an official ChainFunded account.

**The scam:** ChainFunded does not run send-to-receive promotions. Sending any funds to these addresses results in total loss.

**How to stay safe:**
- ChainFunded's official accounts are listed on the official website only
- Never send crypto to receive a larger amount back
- Report fake accounts via the relevant social media platform
