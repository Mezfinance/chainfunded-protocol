## RECITALS

**A.** The Company owns and operates the ChainFunded decentralised prop trading evaluation platform (the "**Platform**"), through which traders pay challenge fees to enter evaluation programmes.

**B.** The Affiliate wishes to promote the Platform and refer prospective traders to it, in exchange for commission payments on qualifying purchases made through the Affiliate's unique referral link.

**C.** The parties enter into this Agreement to record the terms on which such commissions are earned, invoiced, and paid.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1** "**Affiliate Code**" means the unique alphanumeric referral code assigned to the Affiliate by the Company, which is pre-populated in the Affiliate's unique referral link and applied to a trader's session at the point of platform entry.

**1.2** "**Challenge Fee**" means the gross USDC amount paid by a trader to initiate a Phase 1 evaluation challenge on the Platform, as displayed to the trader at the time of payment, before any deduction.

**1.3** "**Commission**" means {{RATE_PCT}}% of the gross Challenge Fee for each Qualified Purchase.

**1.4** "**Commission Period**" means each calendar month during the Term.

**1.5** "**Qualified Purchase**" means a Phase 1 evaluation challenge payment where the Affiliate's Code was applied to the trader's session at the time the on-chain payment transaction was executed, as verified by the Company's Platform records.

**1.6** "**USDC**" means USD Coin, an ERC-20 stablecoin token on the Base blockchain (or such other network as the Platform operates on from time to time).

---

## 2. AFFILIATE OBLIGATIONS

**2.1 Promotion.** The Affiliate shall promote the Platform honestly, accurately, and in a manner that does not misrepresent the Platform's features, risks, returns, or regulatory status.

**2.2 Compliance.** The Affiliate shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and advertising standards in the jurisdictions in which they operate, including without limitation disclosure requirements for paid promotions or affiliate relationships (such as those imposed by the U.S. Federal Trade Commission or equivalent authorities).

**2.3 Prohibited Conduct.** The Affiliate shall not:

  (a) engage in self-referral (using their own Affiliate Code to purchase a challenge for themselves or any entity they control);

  (b) use spam, unsolicited messaging, or other prohibited marketing methods;

  (c) make guarantees of profit or misrepresent evaluation pass rates;

  (d) impersonate the Company or claim to be an employee or officer of the Company;

  (e) use the Affiliate Code in conjunction with any paid advertising campaigns without the Company's prior written consent.

**2.4 Notification.** The Affiliate shall promptly notify the Company if they become aware of any fraudulent use of their Affiliate Code or any misattribution of Qualified Purchases.

---

## 3. COMMISSION

**3.1 Rate.** The Company shall pay the Affiliate a Commission equal to {{RATE_PCT}}% of the gross Challenge Fee for each Qualified Purchase attributable to the Affiliate's Code during a Commission Period.

**3.2 Qualifying Transactions.** Only Phase 1 evaluation challenge purchases constitute Qualified Purchases. Phase 2 challenge fees, renewal fees, payout requests, and any other platform transactions do not qualify.

**3.3 Attribution.** A purchase is attributed to the Affiliate if and only if the Affiliate's Code was applied to the purchasing trader's session at the moment the on-chain payment transaction was confirmed. Attribution is determined solely by the Platform's smart contract and backend records. No retroactive attribution will be made.

**3.4 No Cap.** There is no monthly or annual cap on the total Commission payable to the Affiliate.

**3.5 No Exclusivity.** Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Company from operating other affiliate or referral programmes, or from accepting direct purchases from traders.

---

## 4. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Monthly Invoice.** Within five (5) business days following the end of each Commission Period, the Affiliate shall submit a valid invoice to the Company at **info@mzfprotocol.xyz** (or such other address as the Company notifies in writing) covering the Commissions earned during that Commission Period.

**4.2 Invoice Requirements.** Each invoice must include:

  (a) A unique sequential invoice number;

  (b) The Affiliate's full legal name and address;

  (c) The Commission Period covered (e.g., "1 April 2026 – 30 April 2026");

  (d) The total number of Qualified Purchases during the period;

  (e) The aggregate gross Challenge Fees attributable to those Qualified Purchases (in USDC);

  (f) The Commission amount (being {{RATE_PCT}}% of the figure in (e));

  (g) The Affiliate's USDC wallet address for payment;

  (h) Any applicable tax identification or VAT/GST registration number.

**4.3 Payment.** The Company shall pay each valid invoice within five (5) business days of receipt, by transferring the invoiced Commission amount in USDC to the wallet address specified in the invoice.

**4.4 No Minimum.** There is no minimum invoice amount. The Affiliate may submit an invoice for any Commission amount, including amounts less than one (1) USDC.

**4.5 Disputed Invoices.** If the Company disputes any portion of an invoice, it shall notify the Affiliate within three (3) business days of receipt, identifying the disputed items. The Company shall pay the undisputed portion within the payment period in clause 4.3. The parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any dispute within ten (10) business days.

**4.6 Late Invoices.** Invoices submitted more than sixty (60) days after the end of the relevant Commission Period may be declined by the Company at its reasonable discretion.

## 11. TERM AND TERMINATION

**11.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the date of execution and continues on a month-to-month basis until terminated in accordance with this clause.

**11.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.

**11.3 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party: (a) commits a material breach of this Agreement that is incapable of remedy, or fails to remedy a remediable breach within ten (10) business days of written notice; (b) becomes insolvent, enters into administration, liquidation, or bankruptcy proceedings; or (c) engages in fraud or wilful misconduct.

**11.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination:

  (a) The Affiliate shall immediately cease using the Affiliate Code and all Company brand assets;

  (b) The Company shall remain obligated to pay Commission on all Qualified Purchases that occurred before the effective date of termination, subject to receipt of a valid invoice within thirty (30) days of the termination date;

  (c) Clauses 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13, and 14 survive termination.
