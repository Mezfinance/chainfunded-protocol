## 2. VESTING

**2.1 Vesting Start Date.** The vesting period commences on [DATE] (the "**Vesting Start Date**"), which shall be no later than the date of the TGE.

**2.2 Linear Vesting.** The Vesting Balance of 450,000 tokens vests in equal monthly instalments of **37,500 tokens** on each monthly anniversary of the Vesting Start Date, subject to the Company's Review Right at clause 3.

**2.3 Delivery.** Vested tokens shall be transferred to the Affiliate's designated wallet address (`{{AFFILIATE_WALLET}}`) on or within five (5) business days of each vesting date, or released automatically if a vesting smart contract is used.

**2.4 No Acceleration.** Unless the Company agrees otherwise in writing, vesting does not accelerate on any event, including change of control or termination of C7.

---

## 3. REVIEW RIGHT (TRIAL PERIOD)

**3.1 Review Right.** At any time during the period commencing on the Vesting Start Date and ending on the last day of the third month thereafter (the "**Trial Period**"), the Company may, in its sole and absolute discretion and for any reason or no reason, elect to terminate the remaining vesting schedule by providing written notice to the Affiliate (the "**Review Right**").

**3.2 Effect of Exercise.** If the Company exercises the Review Right:

  (a) all vesting ceases immediately upon the effective date of the written notice;

  (b) the Affiliate retains all tokens already released to them as at that date — being the Initial Unlock and any monthly instalments that have vested and been delivered prior to notice — without any obligation to return them;

  (c) all Allocated Tokens that have not yet vested are forfeited and revert to the Company, without compensation; and

  (d) neither party shall have any further obligation to the other in respect of the Allocated Tokens.

**3.3 Non-Exercise.** If the Company does not exercise the Review Right before the expiry of the Trial Period, the vesting schedule in clause 2.2 continues automatically for the remaining nine (9) monthly instalments (Months 4–12) without further action required by either party.

**3.4 Initial Unlock.** The Initial Unlock of 50,000 tokens released at TGE is not subject to the Review Right and is retained by the Affiliate in all circumstances under this clause.
