# How to Pass Phase 2

To pass Phase 2, reach a 5% profit target on your account while staying within the same daily and total loss limits as Phase 1, and trade on at least 4 separate days.

---

## Phase 2 requirements

| Requirement | Value |
|------------|-------|
| Profit Target | 5% above starting balance |
| Max Daily Loss | 5% of starting balance per day |
| Max Total Loss | 10% of starting balance |
| Min Trading Days | 4 calendar days (UTC) |
| Time Limit | None |

Phase 2 starts with a **fresh account at the same size** — your Phase 1 P&L does not carry over.

---

## How Phase 2 differs from Phase 1

| | Phase 1 | Phase 2 |
|--|---------|---------|
| Profit target | 10% | 5% |
| Loss rules | Same | Same |
| Min trading days | 4 | 4 |
| Fresh start | Yes | Yes |

The lower profit target in Phase 2 is designed to confirm that your Phase 1 result was not a one-off. The drawdown rules are identical.

---

## Step 1: Verify your Phase 2 account is active

On the [Trader Dashboard →](./07-trader-dashboard.md), select your Phase 2 account from the account dropdown. The phase badge should read **"Phase 2"**.

![The account dropdown with the Phase 2 account selected. The phase badge shows "Phase 2" in a distinct colour. The stat cards show a fresh $50,000 balance with $0 P&L.](/screenshots/ch-05-01_phase2-account.png)

---

## Step 2: Apply the same risk habits from Phase 1

The rules are identical — the only change is the lower profit target. Continue using the same approach:

- Review your gauges before each session
- Set stop losses on all positions
- Keep daily risk well within the 5% limit
- Trade at least 4 calendar days before attempting to advance

Note: Traders who pass Phase 2 quickly often do so with fewer, higher-conviction trades rather than overtrading to reach the target faster.

---

## Step 3: Monitor your progress on the dashboard

The phase status banner shows your current profit target progress and days traded. The Profit Target gauge fills as your equity rises toward the 5% goal.

![Dashboard showing Phase 2 progress. Profit Target gauge at 70%. Phase banner shows "Phase 2 — 3 of 4 days traded". Gauges all green.](/screenshots/ch-05-02_phase2-progress.png)

---

## Step 4: Complete Phase 2

When both conditions are met — profit target hit and minimum days satisfied — the dashboard shows a **"Claim Funded Account"** prompt. Select it to finalise Phase 2 and activate your funded account.

![Success modal: "Phase 2 Complete! You are now a funded trader. Select Activate Funded Account to begin earning payouts." A green "Activate Funded Account" button is prominent.](/screenshots/ch-05-03_funded-prompt.png)

You have now successfully passed Phase 2. Your funded account is live and you can request payouts at any time.

---

## What happens after Phase 2

- Your funded account appears in the account dropdown with a **"Funded"** badge
- You can trade the same assets on the same terminal
- A **"Request Payout"** button appears on the dashboard when you have profit
- You keep 80% of all profits, sent on-chain in USDC
