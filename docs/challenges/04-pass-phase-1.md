# How to Pass Phase 1

To pass Phase 1, reach a 10% profit target on your account while staying within the daily and total loss limits, and trade on at least 4 separate days.

---

## Phase 1 requirements

| Requirement | Value |
|------------|-------|
| Profit Target | 10% above starting balance |
| Max Daily Loss | 5% of starting balance per day |
| Max Total Loss | 10% of starting balance |
| Min Trading Days | 4 calendar days (UTC) |
| Time Limit | None |

---

## Step 1: Check your dashboard before each session

Before placing a trade, go to the [Trader Dashboard →](./07-trader-dashboard.md) and review:

- **Daily Loss gauge** — how much of today's 5% limit has been used
- **Total Loss gauge** — how much of the 10% total limit has been used
- **Days traded** — count shown on the phase status banner

![The Trader Dashboard showing the three rule gauges before a trading session. All gauges are in the green zone. The phase banner shows "Phase 1 — 2 of 4 min days traded".](/screenshots/ch-04-01_dashboard-pre-trade.png)

---

## Step 2: Open the Trading Terminal

Select **Trade** or **Terminal** in the navigation bar to open the Trading Terminal.

---

## Step 3: Trade within your limits

Place trades using the [Trading Terminal →](../trading/02-place-a-trade.md). As you trade, monitor the balance bar at the top of the terminal — it shows your live equity, daily P&L, and loss limits.

**Key habits for passing Phase 1:**

- Set a stop loss on every position — this prevents a single trade from breaching your daily limit
- Keep individual position risk below 1–2% of your account size
- Do not over-leverage — the daily loss limit is absolute, not proportional to position size

Note: The daily loss limit resets at midnight UTC. If you are near the limit late in a trading day, consider closing positions and waiting for the reset.

---

## Step 4: Track your profit target progress

The **Profit Target gauge** on the dashboard shows how close you are to the 10% target. The stat card **"Profit Target"** shows the remaining dollar amount needed.

![The dashboard stat cards and gauges with the Profit Target gauge at 60% complete. The "Profit Target" stat card shows "$2,000 remaining" for a $50,000 account.](/screenshots/ch-04-02_profit-target-progress.png)

---

## Step 5: Satisfy the minimum trading days rule

Check the **phase status banner** at the top of the dashboard. It shows how many of the required 4 trading days you have completed. A trading day is any UTC calendar day with at least one closed trade.

![The phase status banner showing "Phase 1 — 3 of 4 minimum trading days completed". A progress bar or counter shows the day count.](/screenshots/ch-04-03_min-days-banner.png)

---

## Step 6: Advance to Phase 2

When both conditions are met — profit target reached and minimum days satisfied — the dashboard displays a **"Advance to Phase 2"** prompt. Select it to confirm the advancement.

![A success banner or modal: "Phase 1 Complete! You have hit your profit target and satisfied all rules. Select Advance to Phase 2 to continue." A green "Advance to Phase 2" button is visible.](/screenshots/ch-04-04_advance-prompt.png)

You have now successfully passed Phase 1. Your Phase 2 challenge starts immediately with a fresh account at the same size.

---

## Common reasons for failure

| Issue | Prevention |
|-------|-----------|
| Daily loss limit violated | Set stop losses; don't chase losses near end of day |
| Total loss limit violated | Reduce position size after drawdowns; never risk more than 2% per trade |
| Overleveraged position wiped out | Use conservative leverage; calculate position size before trading |
