# How to Reset a Failed Challenge

If your challenge account fails — by breaching the daily or total loss limit — you can start a new attempt by paying the evaluation fee again.

---

## What happens when an account fails

When your equity hits the daily loss floor or the total loss floor, the system:

1. Force-closes all open positions at the current market price
2. Marks the account as **"Failed"** in the dashboard
3. Locks the account — no further trading is possible on that account

Your failed account remains visible in the dashboard when you select **"Show Archived"**. The trade history and final balance are preserved for your review.

---

## Step 1: Review what went wrong

Before starting a new attempt, review the failed account's trade history and equity curve on the [Trader Dashboard →](./07-trader-dashboard.md). Understanding what caused the breach helps you avoid repeating it.

Select **Show Archived** near the account selector to make failed accounts visible.

![The Trader Dashboard with a failed account visible. The account shows a red "Failed" badge in the account selector. The rule gauges show the total loss gauge at 100% (fully filled in red). The equity curve shows the drawdown that caused the failure.](/screenshots/ch-06-01_failed-account.png)

Common failure causes:
- Single trade with no stop loss wiped out the daily limit
- Overleveraged position during a volatile period
- Accumulated small losses that hit the total loss limit over time

---

## Step 2: Navigate to Start Challenge

Select **Start Challenge** in the navigation bar. This takes you to the tier selection page.

---

## Step 3: Select an account size

Select the account size tier for your new attempt. You can choose the same size or a different one.

Note: Each attempt is a fresh, independent account. Your previous challenge's P&L, trading history, and phase progress do not carry over.

![The Start Challenge page showing the five account size tier cards. The $50,000 card is selected with a green border.](/screenshots/ch-06-02_tier-select.png)

---

## Step 4: Pay the evaluation fee

Select **Continue to Payment** and complete the fee payment. The process is identical to starting your first challenge. See [How to Start a Challenge →](./03-start-a-challenge.md) for the full walkthrough.

---

## Step 5: Start trading again

Once the transaction confirms, a new Phase 1 account is created and appears in your dashboard. Select it from the account dropdown to begin.

You have now successfully reset your challenge. Your new Phase 1 account is active.

---

## There is no limit on retries

You can start as many challenges as you like. Each attempt is independent and requires a new evaluation fee.
