# How to Close a Position

To close an open position, select **Close** on the position card in the Positions Panel. The position closes instantly at the current market price.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Locate your position in the Positions Panel

The **Positions Panel** at the bottom of the Trading Terminal lists all open positions for the selected account. Find the position you want to close.

![The Positions Panel showing one open trade: BTC/USDT Long, $500 size, entry $67,432, current $68,100, unrealised P&L +$98.40 in green. A "Close" button is on the right of the row.](/screenshots/tr-06-01_positions-panel.png)

---

### Step 2: Select "Close"

Select the **Close** button on the position card. The position closes immediately at the current market price.

![Close-up of the "Close" button on the position card, highlighted.](/screenshots/tr-06-02_close-button.png)

---

### Step 3: Position closed

A success toast appears. The position is removed from the Positions Panel and the P&L is recorded in your trade history and added to your daily and total P&L.

![Toast notification: "Position closed — BTC/USDT Long +$98.40". The Positions Panel is now empty (or shows remaining open positions).](/screenshots/tr-06-04_closed-toast.png)

You have now successfully closed the position. The realised P&L is reflected immediately in the Account Balance Bar and the Trader Dashboard.

---

## Closing via stop loss or take profit

Positions also close automatically when:
- The market price reaches your **Stop Loss** level — closes at a loss
- The Market price reaches your **Take Profit** level — closes at a profit

Automatic closes are recorded in the trade history with the exit type ("Stop Loss" or "Take Profit") noted.
