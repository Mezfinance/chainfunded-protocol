# Common Questions: Platform and Access

---

**Can I use ChainFunded on mobile?**

Yes. The web app is fully mobile-responsive. The Trading Terminal switches to a tab-based layout on small screens — tabs for Chart, Order, and Positions at the bottom of the screen.

To connect a wallet on mobile, use a mobile wallet app (MetaMask Mobile, Coinbase Wallet, Rainbow) and select WalletConnect on the connect screen.

---

**Can I run multiple challenge accounts at the same time?**

Yes. There is no limit on simultaneous challenge accounts. You can hold a Phase 1 account, a Phase 2 account, and a funded account at the same time across different or the same account sizes. Each is fully independent.

---

**What happens if I lose access to my wallet?**

Your challenge accounts, LP position, and affiliate earnings are tied to your wallet address on-chain. ChainFunded does not hold private keys or have any account recovery mechanism.

If you lose wallet access and do not have your seed phrase, the on-chain assets associated with that address are inaccessible. Keep a secure offline backup of your seed phrase. See [How to Keep Your Account Secure →](../security/01-account-security.md).

---

**Can I access my account from multiple devices?**

Yes. Connect the same wallet address on any device and you will see the same challenge accounts, LP position, and affiliate dashboard. The data is on-chain — it is not stored in a browser session.

---

**Is ChainFunded available in all countries?**

ChainFunded is a decentralised application on public blockchain infrastructure — there is no geographic account restriction at the protocol level. However, access may be restricted or regulated in certain jurisdictions. It is your responsibility to check whether using the platform complies with the laws in your country before participating.
