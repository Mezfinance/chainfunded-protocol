# Common Questions: Fees and Payment

---

**Do I pay a separate fee for Phase 2?**

No. The evaluation fee covers both phases. You pay once when starting the challenge — Phase 2 activates automatically when you pass Phase 1. There is no second payment.

---

**Can I pay with a credit or debit card?**

Yes. The Start Challenge page has a **Pay with Card** option powered by Stripe. This lets you purchase USDC and pay the evaluation fee in a single flow without needing USDC already in your wallet.

---

**Where does my evaluation fee go?**

The fee is paid in USDC directly to the ChainFunded protocol smart contract in a single on-chain transaction. The contract automatically allocates it as follows:

| Recipient | Share | Purpose |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **Capital Pool** | ~50–62% | Reserves that fund trader payouts and fee refunds |
| **Protocol Wallet** | 24% | Protocol operations (ChainFunded DAO LLC) |
| **LP Profit Pool** | 6% | Yield distributed to liquidity providers |
| **Software Licence Fee** | Included in protocol share | Paid by the DAO to ChainFunded Labs (MZF-Protocol Inc.) for platform software |
| **Affiliate Commission** | 8–20% | Paid to the referring affiliate (if applicable; only on first challenge per trader) |

**Key points:**

- The capital pool share varies depending on whether an affiliate referred you (8–20% goes to the affiliate, reducing the capital pool portion).
- If you were not referred by an affiliate, the capital pool receives the full remainder after the protocol and LP shares.
- The software licence fee is not a separate deduction from your fee — it is paid by the DAO to ChainFunded Labs from the protocol's 24% share, under the Software License Agreement between the two entities.
- All allocations are enforced by smart contract code and are publicly verifiable on-chain. No human has discretion over how fees are distributed.
- Your evaluation fee is **fully refunded** on your first funded payout, paid automatically by the smart contract from the capital pool.

---

**Is there a discount for buying multiple challenges?**

No standard bulk discount applies. Check the official ChainFunded website for any active promotions.
