# Common Questions: Trading Mechanics

---

**Can I trade outside market hours?**

No. The order panel is disabled when a market is closed. Positions already open remain active and will update when the market reopens, but you cannot open new positions.

Market hours:

| Asset Class | Hours (ET) |
|------------|-------------|
| Crypto | 24/7 |
| Forex | Sun 17:00 – Fri 17:00 |
| Commodities | Sun 18:00 – Fri 17:00 (daily maintenance break 17:00–18:00) |
| US Stocks | Mon–Fri 09:30 – 16:00 |

---

**What is the maximum leverage per asset class?**

Leverage depends on the asset class and, within it, on how liquid the
instrument is:

| Asset Class | Max Leverage |
|------------|-------------|
| Crypto | 10x (large cap), 5x (mid cap) |
| Forex | 100x (majors), 50x (crosses), 30x (exotics) |
| Commodities | 20x (metals), 15x (energy) |
| US Stocks | 5x |

The order panel shows the exact limit for the instrument you're trading.

---

**What order types are available?**

- **Market orders** — execute immediately at the current price
- **Limit orders** — queue to execute when the market reaches a price you specify

Stop loss and take profit are exit levels you can attach to any open position.

---

**Does using high leverage affect my challenge rules?**

The challenge loss limits (5% daily, 10% total) are based on account equity — not position size. Higher leverage makes your equity move faster, so you can reach a limit sooner from the same-size account move. The limits themselves do not change with leverage.

---

**Where do ChainFunded prices come from?**

All asset prices are sourced from institutional market data providers and delivered through ChainFunded's own price service, so the price shown in the terminal is the same one your trades are filled against. Prices update continuously during market hours.

---

**Can I trade multiple challenge accounts at the same time?**

Yes. If you have multiple active challenge accounts (e.g. a Phase 1 and a Phase 2 account simultaneously), you can switch between them using the account dropdown on the Trading Terminal. Each account has its own independent P&L, drawdown tracking, and trading history.
