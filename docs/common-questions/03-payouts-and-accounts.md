# Common Questions: Payouts and Funded Accounts

---

**How long does a payout actually take?**

30–90 seconds from signing. After you sign the payout request in your wallet, the transaction is submitted to the smart contract. Verification and USDC transfer happen within one to two block confirmations on Ethereum Sepolia.

---

**What happens to my funded account balance after a payout?**

Your funded account balance resets to the original starting balance (e.g. back to $50,000 on a $50K account). You continue trading from that reset balance and can request further payouts as you build profit again. There is no limit on the number of payout cycles.

---

**Can I request multiple payouts from the same funded account?**

Yes. Each payout resets your balance to the starting amount. You can trade, accumulate profit, and request again as many times as you like — the funded account remains active indefinitely.

---

**Can I change the wallet address that receives my payouts?**

No. Payouts are sent to the wallet address that paid the evaluation fee and registered the challenge. This is enforced at the smart contract level and cannot be changed after registration.

If you need to use a different address, you would need to start a new challenge from the new wallet.

---

**How do I see my payout history?**

Select **Payouts** in the navigation bar. Every payout is recorded in a table with the date, amount, your 80% share, and an Etherscan link for independent verification. See [Payout History and Status →](../payouts/03-payout-history-and-status.md).
