## 5. ON-CHAIN DATA

**5.1 Public Blockchain Data.** When you interact with the Protocol's smart contracts on the Ethereum blockchain, the following data is permanently and publicly recorded:
- (a) Your wallet address;
- (b) Transaction details (amounts, timestamps, contract interactions);
- (c) Challenge creation and status changes;
- (d) Payout transactions;
- (e) LP deposits and withdrawals;
- (f) Governance votes (if applicable).

**5.2 Immutability.** On-chain data cannot be modified, deleted, or restricted by ChainFunded Labs, the DAO, or any other party. This is an inherent characteristic of public blockchain technology.

**5.3 Pseudonymity.** On-chain data is associated with wallet addresses, not personal identities. However, if your wallet address is linked to your identity through other means (e.g., a centralised exchange KYC process), on-chain data may become personally identifiable.

**5.4 Not Our Responsibility.** ChainFunded Labs does not control the Ethereum blockchain and is not responsible for the permanence or public accessibility of on-chain data.
