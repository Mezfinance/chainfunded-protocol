Usage Guidelines

Keep the brand consistent across all touchpoints.

Do

  • Use the provided SVGs at any scale
  • Maintain clear space around the logo (min 50% of logo height)
  • Use the green bolt on white/light backgrounds
  • Use the white bolt on green/dark backgrounds
  • Use "ChainFunded" as one word, capital C and F

Don't

  • Don't stretch, skew, or rotate the logo
  • Don't change the logo colours
  • Don't add drop shadows, outlines, or effects
  • Don't use the logo smaller than 24px
  • Don't write "Chain Funded", "Chainfunded", or "chain funded"
