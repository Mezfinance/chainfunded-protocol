Brand Relationship

The DAO and the protocol are distinct brands with a shared visual system.

ChainFunded (Protocol)

The trading platform and user-facing product. Uses the lightning bolt logo mark. Operated by ChainFunded Pte. Ltd. (Singapore).

ChainFunded DAO

Protocol governance and stewardship entity. Uses the shield + bolt logo mark. Cayman Islands DAO Company. Holds IP, governs protocol parameters, manages treasury.

Usage Guidelines

Maintain clear distinction between the DAO and the protocol.

Do

  • Use the shield logo for DAO-related contexts
  • Use the bolt logo for protocol/product contexts
  • Write "ChainFunded DAO" in full when referring to the entity
  • Maintain clear space around logos (min 50% of height)
  • Use provided colour values exactly

Don't

  • Don't use the shield logo for the trading product
  • Don't use the bolt logo when referring to the DAO entity
  • Don't abbreviate to "CF DAO" or "CFG DAO"
  • Don't modify, recolour, or add effects to logos
  • Don't imply the DAO operates the trading platform
