On-Chain Prop Trading

About ChainFunded

The world's first fully on-chain prop trading evaluation platform. Rules enforced by code. Payouts executed by smart contracts.

What Is ChainFunded?

ChainFunded lets skilled traders prove their ability and earn funded capital — with every rule, every payout, and every fee split enforced transparently on Ethereum.

The Problem

Traditional prop firms operate as black boxes. Payouts are denied arbitrarily, rules change without notice, and firms collapse overnight. Traders have no way to verify that the firm will actually pay them.

The Solution

ChainFunded replaces trust with code. Smart contracts enforce the rules, execute payouts automatically, and hold capital non-custodially. Every transaction is verifiable on Ethereum. No human discretion. No delays.

How It Works

Three steps from evaluation to funded payouts — all on-chain.

1

Pay & Trade

Choose an account size and pay the one-time evaluation fee in USDC. Trade through two phases — hit the profit targets while respecting the drawdown rules.

2

Pass & Get Funded

Pass both phases and receive a funded account. The smart contract verifies your results cryptographically — no manual review, no forms, no waiting period.

3

Trade & Get Paid

Trade your funded account and keep the majority of your profits, settled directly to your wallet in USDC. Your evaluation fee is refunded on your first payout.

Account sizes, fees, profit splits, and challenge rules are set on-chain and governed by CFG token holders. See the governance portal for current parameters.

For Traders

  • Multiple account sizes — from entry-level to institutional
  • Majority profit split on all funded trading profits
  • Full fee refund on your first funded payout
  • Multi-asset: crypto, FX, and stock indices
  • Automatic USDC settlement — no forms, no delays
  • Wallet-based auth — no email, no passwords, no KYC at protocol level

For Liquidity Providers

  • Deposit USDC into the non-custodial LP pool
  • Earn yield from evaluation fees and funded trader profit-sharing
  • Withdraw at any time (subject to pool liquidity)
  • LP token (CFND) represents your pool position, verifiable on-chain
  • All yield rates determined by protocol fee parameters, governed by CFG holders

The Protocol

Built on production-grade Web3 infrastructure. Every component is transparent and verifiable.

Ethereum

Smart contracts deployed on Ethereum mainnet. Deepest liquidity, strongest security guarantees.

USDC Settlement

All fees, payouts, and LP deposits use USDC — the most regulated stablecoin, issued by Circle.

ECDSA Payouts

Payout proofs are cryptographically signed. The smart contract verifies and executes — no human approval.

Non-Custodial

All funds held in smart contracts, not bank accounts. LPs verify their position on-chain at any time.

OFAC Compliance

3-layer sanctions screening: hardcoded SDN list, Chainalysis on-chain oracle, and Chainalysis REST API.

Governance

Protocol parameters controlled by CFG token holders through on-chain governance with a 2-day timelock.
