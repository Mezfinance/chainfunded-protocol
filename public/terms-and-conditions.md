### 16. CHALLENGE RULES

**16.1 Two-Phase Evaluation.** Each challenge consists of two sequential evaluation phases. You must meet the profit target for each phase while respecting all risk management rules to advance.

**16.2 Challenge Parameters.** The following rules are enforced by the Protocol smart contracts. ChainFunded Labs does not set, modify, or have discretion over these rules:

| Rule | Value |
|------|-------|
| Phase 1 profit target | 10% of account size |
| Phase 2 profit target | 5% of account size |
| Maximum daily loss | 5% of account size |
| Maximum total drawdown | 10% of account size |
| Minimum trading days per phase | 4 days |
| Best single day cap | ≤50% of total profit |
| Profit split (trader) | 80% |
| Profit split (protocol) | 20% |

**16.3 Rule Lock.** Challenge rules are locked at the time of challenge creation. Rules cannot be changed retroactively for an active challenge by the DAO, ChainFunded Labs, or any other party.

**16.4 Automatic Enforcement.** Drawdown limits are monitored continuously. If your account breaches the maximum daily loss or maximum total drawdown limit, your challenge is automatically failed and your positions may be automatically closed by the monitoring system.

**16.5 Best Single Day Cap.** No single trading day's profit may constitute more than 50% of your total profit at the time of phase completion. This rule prevents pass-by-luck scenarios and ensures consistent performance.

**22.3 Trading Rewards (CFGDistributor).** Challenge account purchasers may earn CFG tokens through a season-based reward programme:
- (a) The programme distributes a fixed allocation of CFG tokens across ten sequential seasons, each lasting approximately six months;
- (b) Points are earned proportionally to challenge account size. Phase 2 accounts earn a higher multiplier than Phase 1. Whitelisted (free) accounts earn a lower multiplier than paid accounts;
- (c) After each season ends, participants may claim their pro-rata share of that season's CFG budget;
- (d) All registrations are verified on-chain against the ChainFundedPool smart contract. Fake or unregistered challenges cannot earn points;
- (e) Season budgets follow a front-loaded decay schedule and cannot be modified after creation;
- (f) Earning CFG through trading rewards is not guaranteed. The amount received depends on total participation in each season.

**22.4 LP Staking Rewards (CFGLPRewards).** Liquidity providers may earn CFG tokens by actively staking CFND tokens:
- (a) Staking is an **active opt-in**. Passive CFND holders do not earn CFG rewards;
- (b) CFG is emitted continuously per second to stakers in proportion to their staked balance;
- (c) Stakers continue to earn USDC pool profit on their staked CFND via a proxy harvest mechanism;
- (d) There is no lock-up period. Stakers may unstake at any time, at which point CFG emission to that staker ceases;
- (e) The programme distributes a fixed allocation across ten seasons with a front-loaded decay schedule;
- (f) Earning CFG through staking is not guaranteed. The amount received depends on total staked participation.

**22.5 Governance Participation.** CFG token holders who have delegated their voting power (including self-delegation) may:
- (a) Create governance proposals (subject to the proposal threshold);
- (b) Vote on governance proposals;
- (c) Execute passed proposals through the TimelockController.

**22.6 Vesting Token Delegation.** Beneficiaries of CFGVesting contracts may delegate the voting power of their unvested tokens via the `delegateHeld()` function. This allows governance participation before tokens are fully vested.
